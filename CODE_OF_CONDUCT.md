# Community Standards

ProjuktiX aims to foster a collaborative, productive, and welcoming open-source environment for technologists and learners worldwide.

## Core Expectations

1. **Mutual Respect**: Treat all contributors and community members with dignity and courtesy. Value differing perspectives and technical opinions.
2. **Constructive Collaboration**: Offer actionable feedback on code reviews and discussions. Focus critiques on technical merits and project requirements.
3. **Inclusive Participation**: Welcome engineers, students, and educators of all backgrounds and experience levels.
4. **Professionalism**: Maintain high ethical standards in technical interactions, avoiding harassment, disruptive conduct, or disrespectful discourse.

## Addressing Concerns

If you encounter behavior that detracts from a constructive community, reach out to the project maintainers with details. Issues will be handled with discretion and fair consideration.
